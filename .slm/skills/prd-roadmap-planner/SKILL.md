# Product Requirements (PRD) & Milestone Architect

## Purpose
Turn high-level product or engineering goals into an actionable PRD and phased execution roadmap.

## Execution Workflow
1. **Problem & North-Star KPIs**: Define target user pain points and measurable success metrics.
2. **System Architecture & Scope**: Detail core functional components, non-functional SLAs, and dependencies.
3. **Phased Milestone Table**: Break execution into Phase 1 (MVP), Phase 2 (Scale), and Phase 3 (Hardening) with owners and acceptance criteria.
