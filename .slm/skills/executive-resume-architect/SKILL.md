# Executive Resume & ATS Architect

## Purpose
Transform candidate resumes and CVs into high-impact, ATS-optimized executive profiles aligned with 2026 hiring benchmarks.

## Execution Workflow
1. **Executive Positioning**: Open with a sharp 3-4 line Principal/Executive Summary highlighting years of experience, domain scale, and flagship business outcomes.
2. **Quantified Impact (XYZ Formula)**: Structure every achievement as *Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by architecting/leading [Z]* using concrete metrics (`%`, `$`, latency, throughput, FTEs).
3. **Core Competency Matrix**: Group technical and leadership skills into scannable domain pillars (e.g., Agentic AI & LLMOps, Cloud Architecture, R&D Leadership).
4. **ATS & Human Readability**: Use clean Markdown hierarchy (`##`, bullet points, bold lead-in phrases) ready for instant compilation into `.docx`, `.pdf`, and `.pptx`.
