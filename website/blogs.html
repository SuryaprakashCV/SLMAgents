    Context Distillation for CPU RAG: A Controlled Study on Sub-3B Models

    Most local RAG pipelines retrieve the top 5–8 chunks from a vector database and dump 2,500+ tokens into the prompt. When run on sub-3B models on CPU, prefill latency spikes and hallucination rates surge. We ran a controlled 120-query ablation to demonstrate how cross-encoder distillation to Top-K=2 maximizes grounded accuracy and cuts TTFT by 75%.
